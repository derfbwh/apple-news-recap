This Week in Apple: The Calm Before WWDC—And What's Coming

May 29, 2026

One week remains before WWDC 2026, and Apple has entered the quiet phase of its product cycle. The company has released no major announcements this week, but the silence is deafening. Behind the scenes, supply chain reports and developer leaks paint a picture of an unusually ambitious hardware roadmap—one that suggests Apple is preparing to announce not just software, but potentially several new products as early as next week. Meanwhile, the company continues its work in developer education, marking the fifth commencement of its Detroit academy, which has trained over 1,800 developers since 2021. The week's real story is what we know is coming: iOS 27 with a redesigned Siri, sweeping AI enhancements to the Camera and Photos apps, and the possibility of new Macs, home products, and accessories arriving sooner than expected.

The AI Camera Revolution

The most exciting leak of the week involves the Camera app in iOS 27. The ability to use natural language to edit photos and reframe shots [1] is a glimpse into a future where the distinction between 'taking' a photo and 'creating' one becomes increasingly blurred.

The Hardware Wildcard

While WWDC is usually about software, the sheer number of 'ready-to-ship' products [2] suggests that Tim Cook might have a few hardware surprises up his sleeve this year. An M5 Mac Studio would be a welcome addition for professional users who have been waiting for a significant performance bump.

What to Watch Next Week
