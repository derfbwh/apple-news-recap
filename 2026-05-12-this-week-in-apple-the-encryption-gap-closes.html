This Week in Apple: The Encryption Gap Closes

2026-05-12

The big story this week isn’t just a software update; it’s the quiet closing of one of the most annoying security gaps in the mobile world. Apple released iOS 26.5 on Monday, and with it, the long-promised end-to-end encryption for RCS messaging has finally entered beta. For years, the "green bubble" wasn't just a social stigma—it was a security liability. Now, that’s starting to change. Meanwhile, the shadow of the upcoming leadership transition looms large. With John Ternus set to take the helm in September, every move Apple makes now is being viewed through the lens of the "Ternus Era."
